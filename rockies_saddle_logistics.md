## 2. Chemical Octane Booster Dosing Protocol (Lucas Oil MMT Formula)

When 91 Premium is unavailable at remote pumps along Northern Highway 11 (between Longlac and Hearst) or isolated prairie outposts:
* **Dosage Rate**: **2.08 ml per liter of fuel per octane number**.
* **Boosting 87 AKI to 91 AKI (+4 numbers) in Ducati's 20L Tank**:
  $$\text{Required Dose} = 20\text{ L} \times 4 \times 2.08\text{ ml/L} = \mathbf{166.4\text{ ml (5.6 fl oz)}}$$
* **Partial Tank Top-up (10 Liters 87 Regular)**:
  $$\text{Required Dose} = 10\text{ L} \times 4 \times 2.08\text{ ml/L} = \mathbf{83.2\text{ ml (2.8 fl oz)}}$$
* **Execution**: Inject booster using a graduated dosing syringe directly into the Ducati's tank **BEFORE** pumping fuel. The incoming high-velocity fuel stream guarantees instantaneous molecular mixing.
